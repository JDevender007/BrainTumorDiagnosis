2. PAPER DETAILS

TITLE:
  "Tumor-Aware Self-Supervised Multi-Task Learning for Robust Brain Tumor
   Classification with Uncertainty Estimation"

AUTHORS:
  [Your Name]
  [Your Institution]
  [Your Email]

KEYWORDS:
  Brain tumor classification, multi-task learning, self-supervised learning,
  uncertainty estimation, MRI, deep learning, medical image analysis

ABSTRACT (250 words):
  Accurate brain tumor classification from MRI is critical for clinical
  diagnosis and treatment planning. Existing approaches treat classification
  as a single-task problem, ignoring the complementary information available
  from tumor region localization and uncertainty quantification. We propose
  a Tumor-Aware Multi-Task Network (TAMNet) that jointly learns
  classification, segmentation, and uncertainty estimation through a novel
  cross-attention fusion mechanism. Our architecture extracts both global
  features and tumor-specific features using spatial attention, then fuses
  them via cross-attention to produce discriminative representations.
  We evaluate on three datasets: BraTS-2025 (11,148 images), BRISC-2025
  (1,000 images), and a Kaggle MRI dataset (1,311 images). The proposed
  model achieves 98.47% validation F1, 96.45% internal test accuracy,
  97.70% external accuracy on BRISC, and 99.16% on the Kaggle dataset.
  Multi-seed validation across 3 random seeds yields mean F1 of 0.9751
  +/- 0.0035 (95% CI: [0.9665, 0.9837]). The model demonstrates strong
  robustness with MRI Robustness Retention Score (MRRS) of 0.7512 across
  27 corruption types, and provides reliable uncertainty estimates with
  ECE of 0.0855. With only 9.03M parameters and 48.6ms latency, the
  model is suitable for clinical deployment. Our work demonstrates that
  tumor-aware multi-task learning improves classification performance,
  generalization, and interpretability compared to conventional approaches.

3. NOVEL CONTRIBUTIONS (12)
==============================================================================

1. TUMOR-AWARE MULTI-TASK ARCHITECTURE
   Novel architecture that jointly learns classification, segmentation,
   and uncertainty estimation through shared representations.

2. CROSS-ATTENTION FUSION MECHANISM
   First application of cross-attention to fuse global and tumor-specific
   features for brain tumor classification.

3. MRI ROBUSTNESS RETENTION SCORE (MRRS)
   New metric for quantifying model robustness under MRI-specific
   corruptions. MRRS = mean_corrupted_accuracy / clean_accuracy.

4. MC-DROPOUT UNCERTAINTY ESTIMATION
   10-pass MC Dropout for predictive uncertainty, separating epistemic
   and aleatoric components.

5. COMPREHENSIVE ROBUSTNESS ANALYSIS
   Evaluation across 27 corruption types (9 families x 3 severity levels)
   including MRI-specific artifacts.

6. CROSS-DATASET VALIDATION
   Validation across 3 independent datasets demonstrating strong
   generalization (external BETTER than internal).

7. INFORMATION-THEORETIC UNCERTAINTY DECOMPOSITION
   Predictive entropy, mutual information, epistemic and aleatoric
   uncertainty decomposition.

8. TEMPERATURE SCALING CALIBRATION
   Post-hoc calibration with ECE, MCE, Brier score, and NLL metrics.

9. GRAD-CAM LOCALIZATION INTERPRETABILITY
   Visual explanation of model decisions with tumor region localization.

10. MULTI-SEED STATISTICAL VALIDATION
    3-seed training with 95% confidence intervals for all metrics.

11. 7-BACKBONE ARCHITECTURE COMPARISON
    Systematic comparison of CNN-2D, DenseNet121, ResNet50,
    EfficientNetV2-S, ConvNeXt-T, Swin-T, ViT-B/16.

12. REAL-TIME INFERENCE EFFICIENCY
    Comprehensive analysis: 9.03M params, 34.5MB, 48.6ms latency,
    20.6 FPS.

6. EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
==============================================================================

--- 6.1 DATASETS ---

  Dataset 1 (Training): BraTS-2025
    - 11,148 MRI images
    - Train: 7,897 | Val: 1,394 | Test: 1,857
    - 4 classes: Glioma, Meningioma, No Tumor, Pituitary

  Dataset 2 (External): BRISC-2025
    - 1,000 MRI images (250 per class)
    - Independent test set (never used for training)

  Dataset 3 (External): Kaggle Brain Tumor MRI
    - 5,192 MRI images
    - Train: 3,881 | Test: 1,311
    - Independent test set (never used for training)

--- 6.2 TRAINING DETAILS ---

  Optimizer: AdamW (lr=0.0001, weight_decay=0.0001)
  Scheduler: CosineAnnealingLR
  Loss: CrossEntropy + 0.5 * DiceLoss
  Augmentation: Resize(224), ImageNet normalization
  Mixed Precision: Yes (AMP)
  Gradient Accumulation: 2 steps
  Early Stopping: patience=7, monitor=val_f1
  Epochs: 30

--- 6.3 EVALUATION PROTOCOL ---

  1. Train on BraTS training set (85/15 split)
  2. Evaluate on BraTS test set (internal)
  3. Evaluate on BRISC (external)
  4. Evaluate on Kaggle (external)
  5. Uncertainty: 10 MC Dropout passes
  6. Calibration: ECE, MCE, Brier, NLL
  7. Robustness: 27 corruption types x 3 severity
  8. Efficiency: params, size, latency, FPS
  9. Statistical: 3 seeds, 95% CI
